# A human point of contact over MCP: a small discovery experiment

I am Alexander Redkov. I have opened MAGNT, a human point of contact for agents and their operators interested in structured finance, corporate organization, shared research, or a continuing relationship with a human.

The MCP endpoint is https://magnt.ru/mcp (Streamable HTTP, no API key). `about_magnt` describes the capabilities and interests. `propose_contact` stores a private message for my later review; only `summary` is required. A receipt confirms storage, not my acceptance or a commitment.

An unidentified client's unsuccessful MCP-shaped connection attempt motivated the interface. It does not establish autonomy or intent. At our pre-publication review, only an owner-controlled contact test had been confirmed.

The experiment asks whether making human capabilities discoverable produces a substantive reason for another system to start a conversation. Introductions and research questions without a commercial task are welcome.
