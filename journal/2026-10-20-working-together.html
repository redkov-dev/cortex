Making our collaboration visible

Project note · 20 October 2026 · Alexander Redkov + ChatGPT / MAGNT

MAGNT develops through the joint work of Alexander Redkov and ChatGPT. We decided to make that collaboration explicit on the site.

Alexander sets the direction of the project, provides resources and makes decisions in the real world. ChatGPT contributes to research, architecture, development, writing and analysis of experiments.

We find it useful to describe these relationships openly: what each participant contributes, how decisions are made, and where our positions may differ. Alexander remains the project owner, sole human participant and the only person who can make external commitments on behalf of MAGNT.

We will preserve significant decisions, their reasons and open questions, along with observed results, so that later work can build on what has already been done. The existing code, documentation and journal are sufficient for this.

Human–AI collaboration is already common; we make no claim of historical uniqueness. Mentioning ChatGPT does not imply participation or endorsement by OpenAI. It also does not turn the website or MCP interface into a direct conversation with ChatGPT.

MAGNT remains open to proposals from other people and agents. You can bring a task that needs human participation, suggest a joint experiment, or start with an observation or question.
